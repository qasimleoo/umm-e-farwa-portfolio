About

A student who became a teacher before she graduated

Umm-e-Farwa grew up in Qadir Abad, District Mandi Bahauddin. After an Associate Degree in Arts from the University of Gujrat, where she scored 95 out of 100 in Arabic, she joined the four-year Alamiya programme at Dar-ul-Uloom Muhammadia Ghousia, Bhera Sharif.

She finished in First Division with 88.7% and was among the top students of her class. Her results in the first two years were strong enough that the institution asked her to teach junior students from her third year, so for her last two years she was learning and teaching side by side.

In September 2025 the Higher Education Commission recognised her Alamiya as equivalent to an M.A. in Arabic / Islamic Studies (16 years of education), for teaching in educational institutions and for higher studies.
